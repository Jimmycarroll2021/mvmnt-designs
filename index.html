Technical & Commercial FAQ

Direct answers on our craftsmanship, development timelines, EHR integrations, and fixed pricing.

Why do you reject WordPress and off-the-shelf page builders? +
WordPress and site builders like Elementor rely on complex PHP monoliths, MySQL database queries, and 30+ plugin dependencies. This causes sluggish mobile load times, frequent plugin breakages, and endless security vulnerabilities. By writing clean, hand-crafted semantic HTML5, modern CSS3, and compiling to edge servers, our platforms load in under 150ms and never break.
How can you launch a bespoke platform in 7–14 days when agencies take 4 months? +
Traditional agencies lose months in account management, middleman meetings, and bloated bureaucracy. At MVMNT Studio, you work directly with senior technical directors. We utilize focused, distraction-free sprints with pre-engineered architectural foundations, allowing us to ship bespoke, high-performance platforms in days rather than months.
How do integrations with Cliniko, Halaxy, and Calendly work? +
We integrate directly with leading healthcare and practice management platforms without clunky iframe reloads. Prospective clients enjoy a frictionless booking experience that syncs seamlessly with your real-time practitioner schedules while maintaining strict privacy compliance.
What happens after launch? How do we update content or text? +
All code is modular and documented. You can update content via our $395/month Studio Care retainer, where you simply email our engineering team and we push tested, verified changes live within 24 hours. You never have to worry about breaking your site or managing CMS updates.
Are your project prices fixed or will there be scope creep? +
All our sprint packages ($4,800, $8,800, and $16,500+) are 100% fixed-price with clearly defined deliverables. What we agree upon before kickoff is exactly what you pay.

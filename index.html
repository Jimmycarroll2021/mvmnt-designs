TECHNICAL CASE STUDY · HALI INSTITUTE

Architecture & Clinical UX Transformation

Live Project: https://haliinstitute.vercel.app/ · Client: Jayne Halikiotis, Occupational Therapist

1. The Clinical Challenge: Patients with autoimmune illness, lupus, and chronic fatigue suffer severe neuro-cognitive overload. Conventional web templates crammed with unchunked paragraphs caused visitors to bounce before completing discovery calls.
2. The Architectural Solution: We engineered a 4-domain categorized Whole-Person Care Menu (5 + 5 + 4 + 4 focus areas), eliminated all fake div overlays with native HTML5 <dialog> elements, embedded licensed typography (PP Fraktion), and applied strict WCAG 2.1 AAA contrast pairings (up to 17.96:1).
3. Automated CI/CD & Testing: Every update passed an automated headless Playwright test suite verifying 0px horizontal overflow across 6 screen sizes (320px to 1440px), zero console errors, and byte-for-byte SHA-256 parity across mirror endpoints.
